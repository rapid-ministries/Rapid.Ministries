What we do

Hands-on help, right when it matters.

We go where the need is greatest in the days after a major storm, and we stay useful long after the headlines fade.

Rapid Response

Because ministry is our full-time work, we can pack up and head out as soon as a disaster strikes, anywhere in the country.

Cleanup & Tree Removal

Downed trees, debris, and wreckage: we clear the way so families can get back into their homes and start recovering.

Repairs & Rebuilding

When the emergency phase is over, we put our construction skills to work helping people rebuild what the storm took.

Partnering with Others

We love joining forces with churches and groups who want to share Jesus' love. More hands means more people helped.

Our story

A family, a truck, and a calling.

God put it on our hearts to go and serve people in the hardest moments after a disaster. So we made it our full-time work, living on the road and following the need from one community to the next.

  • Answering the callWe began traveling the country to help with tree removal, cleanup, and repairs.
  • 2022: A game-changerBy God's provision, we bought a skid loader, and it multiplied the work we can do on every site.
  • What's nextWe're praying to bring more heavy equipment and more skilled volunteers to every disaster zone we serve.
As each has received a gift, use it to serve one another.
